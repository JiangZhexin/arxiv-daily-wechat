    🌐 English Abstract

    Hypercube graphs are fundamental model spaces of positive curvature in discrete comparison geometry. Its fibers are regular, have similar structure with hypercubes, and are Laplacian-cospectral with hypercubes, although they need not themselves be hypercubes. As a consequence, if the vertex degree in $G$ is constant along each canonical fiber, then every fiber is a hypercube and $G$ is a hypercube bundle. Conversely, for every $d\geq 4$, we construct Lichnerowicz-sharp graphs with non-hypercube canonical fibers of degree $d$.
